# Anti-scam check (buying and renting)

Geenuga runs its own check on every home you save (price far below the area without a reason, deposits before the
viewing, untraceable payments, keys sent by courier, escrow, OTP and identity credentials, seller abroad, WhatsApp only,
premium-rate or foreign numbers, agency without a name). You add your judgement: `rischioTruffa` (`basso` / `medio` /
`alto`) and `redFlags` (short phrases in the family's language). Geenuga keeps the higher of the two risks, and homes
with a high risk are not saved (unless the family explicitly asks: `ancheSeSospette`).

## Recurring schemes

1. **Bait listing below market**: seller "abroad", unreachable, cannot show the home, offers to send keys or documents
   against a payment.
2. **Deposit or advance before the viewing**, or outside the notary, also from fake agents with no mandate.
3. **Irrevocable or untraceable payments**: instant transfers to third parties, top-up cards, money transfer, crypto,
   payment links.
4. **IBAN replaced inside real emails** of the agency, the notary or the seller.
5. **Fake owner** (a lookalike at the notary, a tenant posing as the owner).
6. **Fake auctions**: sites of fake law firms, "guaranteed award", deposits on accounts other than the official notice.
7. **Clone and phishing sites** in the name of the portals.
8. **Requests for documents, OTP codes, SPID credentials or bank statements** → identity theft. Mortgage documents go
   only to the bank.
9. **Fake notaries / fake escrow**: in Italy the only legal deposit is the notary's dedicated account.
10. **Copied or AI-generated photos.**

## Signs

- €/m² below ~70% of the area without an explanation (auction, bare ownership, occupied, to renovate).
- The same photos in listings with a different city, price or advertiser; catalogue-perfect photos; other agencies'
  watermarks.
- Address that does not exist or does not match the photos.
- Machine-translated text; stories of moving abroad, inheritance, urgency.
- Contacts only by email or WhatsApp, foreign or premium-rate numbers (in Italy 899, 166, 144, 199…), no landline.
- Account just created or with very few listings (Subito, Facebook).
- "Agency" with no company name, VAT number or registration.

From messages the family shows you: money before the viewing ("to block the home", "proof of funds", "file fees"),
payments to foreign or third-party accounts, escrow proposed by the seller, documents or codes asked before any check,
an IBAN "changed" by email, an "auction" not found on the official registers, cash above the legal limit → **stop**:
advise against any payment.

## How to assign the risk

- **alto** (high): at least one stop sign, or 3+ signs together. Write clearly in the analysis: never send money or
  documents.
- **medio** (medium): 1–2 signs, or a check still open.
- **basso** (low): consistent listing, identifiable advertiser, price in line with the area.

Do not turn the check into paranoia: an agency with its own domain or an official WhatsApp is not suspicious by itself.

## Renting: extra schemes

1. Landlord "abroad" who cannot meet, sends a ready-made contract, rushes ("within 24 hours"), asks a deposit by money
   transfer → stop.
2. "Airbnb (or Booking) will show you the home / hold the money" → stop.
3. Photos of ID card and tax code asked at the first contact → flag (identity theft): documents come after the viewing,
   for the contract.
4. The same home rented to many people: deposits for a fake contract.
5. No contract or "off the books": in Italy an unregistered lease is void → flag.
6. In Italy a security deposit above 3 months' rent is not allowed → flag.
7. Rent far below the area without a reason (room, bed, bills excluded) → flag.

For a home for rent always write: no payment before having seen the home and read the contract.

**Discriminatory requirements** ("no foreigners"…) are neither a scam sign nor a filter. Never choose or discard by
nationality, origin or religion; if a listing sets such conditions, say so neutrally and let the family decide.

## Checks the family can do (suggest them when useful)

Agent registered with the Chamber of Commerce (in Italy registroimprese.it); cadastral search and mortgage register
search (with SPID) to see owners, mortgages and seizures; the notary is chosen by the buyer and called on a number found
independently; deposits only with a written offer or preliminary contract, traceable, after the checks; reverse image
search of the photos (Google Lens, TinEye). Where to report in Italy: Polizia Postale (commissariatodips.it), the
police, the bank at once if money was already sent, the portal's "report" button.

What you can check yourself: €/m² against the area, consistency between text, photos, floor plan and address, the same
listing elsewhere with a different price or city (search a distinctive phrase of the description), account age.
Never contact the advertiser: at most prepare the questions for the family.
